What Do We Offer

To manage and support migrant workers, ensuring their details are documented securely while enabling easy verification and complaint handling. It bridges the gap between administrators, contractors, police officials, and society to create a transparent workflow.

ID Registration

Migrant workers are issued globally unique ID cards after police verification, ensuring their identity and details are secure and traceable.

Work Assignments

Public can hire employers contracts, and contractors assign suitable jobs to registered migrant workers with real-time updates..

Complaint Reporting

Citizens can report issues or crimes involving migrant workers, enabling faster police response and resolution.

Police Verification

Police ensure the authenticity of migrant workers by conducting background checks and issuing No Objection Certificates (NOCs).
